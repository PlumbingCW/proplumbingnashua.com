Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Nashua, NH

A burst pipe can put gallons a minute into your walls. We stop the water, find the break, repair or replace the pipe and help you with frozen lines before they split.

Same-day appointments when availableAfter-hours calls answeredNashua and nearby towns

Burst Pipe: First Ten Minutes

Our 24/7 line is open, so call as soon as you can, but shut off the water first. Turn the main shutoff valve clockwise, usually in the basement near where the line enters. Open a faucet on the lowest floor to drain the line and one upstairs to relieve pressure. Flip off power to wet areas at the breaker, but only if you can reach the panel without standing in water.

Move belongings out of the wet area, take photos for insurance and soak up what you can. Tell us where the water is coming from, how fast, and whether the main valve stopped it. We offer same-day appointments when the schedule allows, and a burst pipe is the call we move to the front.

Frozen Pipes in Nashua Winters

A frozen pipe is a burst pipe waiting to happen. If a faucet runs slow or stops in cold weather, a pipe may be frozen. Keep the faucet open, open cabinet doors on exterior walls and warm the area with a hair dryer or a space heater kept at a safe distance. Do not use an open flame.

If the pipe is already split, you will see water when it thaws. Shut the main valve at once and call us. Frozen pipes often occur in exterior walls, unheated basements, crawlspaces, garages and under kitchen sinks on cold outside walls.

  • ✓Disconnect hoses from outdoor faucets in the fall.
  • ✓Insulate pipes in unheated spaces.
  • ✓Let faucets drip on the coldest nights.
  • ✓Keep the heat on if you travel, set to a safe level.
  • ✓Know where your main valve is.

How We Repair a Burst Pipe

1

Stop the water

We confirm the main is off and isolate the damaged section.

2

Find the break

We open the smallest access needed and find the failure.

3

Repair or replace

We cut out the damaged section and replace it with new pipe and fittings.

4

Test and restore

We pressure test the repair, restore water and check the rest of the line.

A burst pipe often means other sections have stressed. We check nearby runs and tell you if more of the line is at risk. If the damage is wide, a repiping job may make more sense than patch after patch.

After the Water Stops

Once the pipe is fixed, water damage is the next problem. Wet drywall, insulation and flooring need to dry fast to avoid mold. Call your insurance company, document the damage with photos and ask a water damage company about drying. We repair the plumbing, but we do not claim to dry out a house.

If you are not sure where water is going, our leak detection page explains how we find hidden leaks. If a burst pipe is tied to a heating failure, see our boiler repair page.
