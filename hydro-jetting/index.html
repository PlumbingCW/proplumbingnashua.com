Emergency Hydro Jetting in Nashua, NH

Hydro jetting scours the inside of a drain line with high pressure water, clearing grease, scale and roots that a cable leaves behind. We use it when a drain keeps coming back.

If You Are Dealing With a Backup Tonight

Our 24/7 line is open. If sewage is coming up in a tub or floor drain, stop using water, keep people and pets out of the wet area and call us. We often start with a cable to restore flow, then recommend hydro jetting once the line is moving, because the cable opens a hole and the jetter cleans the pipe.

After-hours jetting depends on the equipment and the pipe. We tell you on the phone what we can do tonight and what is better after a camera look in the morning, so nobody pays for a cleaning that the pipe cannot take.

What Hydro Jetting Does

A hydro jetter feeds a hose with a specialized nozzle into the drain. The nozzle sprays high pressure water forward to break the blockage and backward to pull the hose forward and wash the pipe wall. The result is a clean interior surface instead of a single hole through the clog.

Grease

Kitchen and restaurant lines collect grease that hardens on the wall. Jetting removes it where a cable only pushes through the middle.

Scale and buildup

Mineral and soap scale narrows pipe over time. Jetting restores the diameter.

Roots

Fine roots entering at joints can be cut and washed out. Roots that return mean a joint needs repair.

Sand and silt

Debris from older pipe or backfill settles in low spots. Jetting flushes it toward the sewer.

When We Do Not Recommend It

Hydro jetting is not for every pipe. Fragile, collapsed or severely cracked pipe can be damaged by high pressure water. That is why we recommend a sewer inspection first for older lines. If the camera shows a break, jetting would make the problem worse, and the right call is a sewer line repair.

Older clay and cast iron lines in Nashua neighborhoods that date to the early or mid twentieth century may be fine for jetting or may be brittle. We do not guess. We look, then we decide.
