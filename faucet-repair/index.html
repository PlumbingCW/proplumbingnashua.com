Emergency Faucet Repair in Nashua, NH

Dripping spouts, leaks under the sink, weak flow and handles that will not turn. We repair or replace kitchen, bathroom, laundry and outdoor faucets and the shutoff valves beneath them.

A Faucet Leak Can Wait, a Spray Cannot

Our 24/7 line is open. If water is spraying from a faucet or the supply connection beneath the sink, close the shutoff valve under the sink by turning it clockwise. If there is no shutoff or it will not turn, close the main water valve. Dry the area, and call us.

A slow drip can wait for a normal appointment, but we offer same-day visits when the schedule allows. Repair is quick for most faucets, and a drip adds to your bill every month.

Faucet Problems We Fix

Drips from the spout

A worn cartridge, washer, seat or O-ring. We replace the part and test.

Leaks at the handle

Worn O-rings or a loose packing nut. Often a short repair.

Water under the sink

A loose supply line, a failed shutoff valve or a corroded connection. We trace it before we repair it.

Low flow

A clogged aerator, mineral buildup or a failing cartridge.

Handle will not turn

A frozen cartridge or a stripped stem. We replace the part or the faucet.

Outdoor faucet drips

A worn washer or a failed frost-free stem. Important to fix before the first hard freeze.

Repair or Replace?

A faucet with a worn cartridge is a good repair, and a faucet with a corroded body is a better replacement. If the finish is pitted, the faucet is more than a decade old and parts are no longer available, replacement can cost less than a repair that will not last. We bring parts for common brands and tell you straight when a replacement is better.

If you are replacing a kitchen faucet, we also check the supply lines and shutoff valves, since old valves under a new faucet are a common source of later leaks. A sink that also has a slow drain may need our drain cleaning service.

Outdoor Faucets and Winter in Nashua

Hose bibs freeze in New Hampshire winters. A hose left attached traps water in the faucet and the pipe behind the wall, and a freeze can crack the pipe or the faucet body. Disconnect hoses in the fall, and if your faucet is not a frost-free type, we can replace it. If water drips inside the wall in spring, shut off the supply to that faucet and call.

If a pipe has frozen or burst, go to our burst pipe repair page for the emergency steps.

Faucets and Local Water

Faucets in Nashua homes on Pennichuck water see soft water, which is gentler on aerators and cartridges. In Hudson, hardness is higher at about 57 ppm in the town well, so aerators clog sooner. On private wells in Hollis, iron and hardness vary, and aerator buildup can be fast. Where we see mineral buildup, we recommend cleaning the aerator at a regular interval and tell you how.

What We Bring to a Faucet Visit

Our plumbers carry cartridges, washers, O-rings, aerators, supply lines and shutoff valves for common brands, so most repairs finish in one visit. If your faucet is a specialty model with parts that must be ordered, we tell you before we start and offer a temporary fix, such as closing the leaking valve, so water is not wasted while you wait.

After the repair, we run the faucet hot and cold, check under the sink for dampness and show you how to shut the supply valves. A faucet repair that leaves a wet cabinet is not finished.

Water Efficiency Note

The EPA WaterSense program labels bathroom faucets that use 1.5 gallons per minute or less. If your faucet needs replacing rather than repairing, that label is a simple way to compare models.
