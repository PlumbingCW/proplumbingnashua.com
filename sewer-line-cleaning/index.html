Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Nashua, NH

When the main line stops, every drain in the house is affected. We clean the building sewer from the cleanout, restore flow and tell you what we found.

Main Line Backup: Do This First

Our 24/7 line is open, so call as soon as you see signs of a main line problem. Stop running water, because every gallon you add has nowhere to go. Locate your main cleanout, usually a capped pipe in the basement or outside near the foundation, and keep the area clear so we can get to it.

If wastewater is coming out of a floor drain or the lowest tub, keep everyone away from the area, and do not use bleach or chemicals on it. We offer same-day appointments when the schedule allows and handle after-hours calls for main line backups every week.

How We Clean a Main Line

We open the main cleanout and run a heavy duty cable with a cutting head through the line. The cutter breaks up grease, paper, roots and debris. Once flow is restored, we flush with water to confirm the line is moving, and we check the cleanout for signs of a deeper problem.

  • ✓Cable cleaning for blockages and roots.
  • ✓Hydro jetting for grease, scale and repeat clogs, after a camera check on older pipe.
  • ✓Camera follow up when the cause is not obvious or the problem returns.
  • ✓A written recommendation if the line needs repair.

See our hydro jetting page for when jetting beats a cable, and our sewer inspection page for the camera step.

What Causes Main Line Clogs

Roots

Roots enter at joints and grow into a mass. They return unless the joint is repaired.

Grease

Grease cools in the pipe and builds up on the wall until the line narrows.

Wipes and paper

Flushable wipes do not break down. They snag on roots or rough pipe and build a net.

Collapse or offset

A damaged pipe catches debris at the same spot, again and again.

Storm Drains and Outdoor Lines

Not every sewer call is a sanitary line. Yard drains, driveway trench drains and downspout lines can clog with leaves, silt and roots, and they flood a basement during heavy rain. We clean those too. If the line ties into the building sewer, we say so, because it affects how and where we clean.

In Nashua, runoff from heavy rain and snowmelt moves toward the Nashua and Merrimack rivers and Salmon Brook, and low spots in older neighborhoods can pond. If a yard drain is slow before a storm, call early, so it can be cleared before the rain arrives.

Sewer Cleaning Around Nashua

Main line clogs in Nashua can come from roots in lines that run under mature trees, from grease and paper building up or from wipes. The age of a neighborhood is only a clue. Whichever home you have, the first step is the same, we open the cleanout and find where the line stops.

We also serve Hudson, Merrimack and Hollis. In Hollis, where septic is common, a slow line can mean a full tank or a failing leach field rather than a clog. We check the tank and tell you if it needs pumping.
