Emergency Plumbing in Nashua, NH

A plumbing emergency does not wait for office hours. Pipes split in January cold snaps, water heaters fail on weekends and main drains back up on holidays. Our line is open 24/7, and we send a plumber from 10 Technology Way in Nashua with same-day appointments offered when the schedule allows.

When you call, we ask a few questions that save time. Where is the water coming from? Have you found the main valve? Which fixtures are affected? From the answers, we tell you what to shut off and what to leave alone. If the problem needs a specialist, such as a well pump or a gas utility, we say so.

Plumbing Built Around Older Homes and Newer Ones

Nashua has housing from every era, from mill-era buildings near the Millyard and Downtown to subdivisions off Amherst Street and in South Nashua. Older homes may have galvanized steel, cast iron drains and mixed repairs. Newer homes use PEX, copper and plastic. We ask about the house on the phone so the right parts are on the truck.

Nashua is on municipal sewer and most homes use Pennichuck Water Works, whose Nashua system draws from the Merrimack River and Pennichuck Brook. Pennichuck reports soft water, around 21 ppm hardness, so scale is a smaller issue here than in many places. See our leak detection page if your bill has climbed without a reason.

Rules and Licensing in New Hampshire

The City of Nashua adopts the International Plumbing Code with local amendments, and the Department of Building Safety is where you ask about permits. New Hampshire licenses plumbers and gas fitters through the Mechanical Safety and Licensing Board, and you should verify any contractor you hire. We ask the building office what applies to your address before work that needs a permit.
