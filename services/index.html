Plumbing Services in Nashua, NH

Drains, Sewers and Cleaning

Slow sinks, backups and repeat clogs. Start with drain cleaning and move to jetting or a camera if the clog returns.

Drain Cleaning

Slow sinks, clogged tubs and backed-up main drains cleared by cable or jetter.

Hydro Jetting

High pressure water cleaning for grease, scale and root-clogged lines.

Sewer Line Cleaning

Main sewer and storm drain cleaning to restore flow and prevent backups.

Sewer Inspection

Video camera inspection of drain and sewer lines before you buy, repair or dig.

Sewer Line Repair

Camera-confirmed repair and replacement of damaged building sewers, including pipe lining.

Water Heaters and Heating

Hot water that has stopped, tanks that leak and boilers that will not heat.

Water Heater Repair

No hot water, leaks, strange noises and tripped pilots on tank and tankless units.

Water Heater Replacement

Removal of a failed tank and installation of the right replacement, often same day.

Water Heater Installation

New and upgraded water heater installs, planned around your home and household.

Tankless Water Heater

Tankless install, repair and descaling flushes for on-demand hot water.

Boiler Repair

No-heat boiler calls, leaks, noises and pressure problems on hydronic systems.

Leaks, Pipes and Water Lines

Hidden leaks, burst pipes and the line that brings water to the house.

Leak Detection

Finding hidden leaks, slab leaks and high-bill water losses without tearing out walls.

Burst Pipe Repair

Emergency shutoff, repair and thaw help for burst and frozen pipes.

Water Service Line Repair and Replacement

Repiping

Plumbing Installation

Toilet Repair and Installation

Faucet Repair

Garbage Disposal Repair and Installation

Basement Waterproofing

Commercial Plumbing

Plumbing service for restaurants, shops, offices and multi-unit buildings.

How to Choose the Right Service

If one fixture is slow, start with a drain or a fixture repair. If several fixtures are slow or a floor drain backs up, think main line and read about sewer line cleaning. If a bill climbs for no reason, read about leak detection.

We explain options in plain terms and give a clear price before work starts. If a problem needs another trade, such as an electrician or a well specialist, we say so. Call (603) 815-9179 any time and we will point you to the right page.
