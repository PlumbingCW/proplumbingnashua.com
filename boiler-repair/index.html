Emergency Boiler Repair in Nashua, NH

No heat on a cold New Hampshire night is an emergency. We diagnose and repair boilers, circulator pumps, valves and the piping around them.

If You Have No Heat Tonight

Our 24/7 line is open, and a no-heat call in winter is our priority. In a New Hampshire cold snap, a house without heat can see pipes freeze within hours, so call early rather than hoping it restarts. While you wait, check the thermostat, the emergency switch near the boiler and the pressure gauge, and tell us what they show.

If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. If you see water leaking around the boiler, shut the cold feed to it and tell us. Open cabinet doors on exterior walls, let faucets drip to keep water moving, and keep warm in one room. We offer same-day appointments when the schedule allows.

Boiler Problems We Diagnose

No heat or hot water

Ignition failure, a tripped safety, a failed circulator or no fuel. The cause may be simple or serious.

Banging or kettling

Scale or sludge on the heat exchanger, or air in the system.

Pressure too high or low

A failed expansion tank, a leaking relief valve or a slow leak that has dropped the pressure.

Leaks

Weeping valves, failing gaskets, cracked fittings or a failing boiler body.

Cold radiators or zones

Trapped air, failed zone valves or circulator trouble.

Short cycling

The boiler turns on and off quickly, often from oversizing, a bad thermostat or a clogged system.

Repair or Replace

Many boiler problems are repairs: a circulator, a zone valve, a pressure relief valve, an expansion tank or a bleed. A boiler with a cracked heat exchanger or a leaking body is beyond repair. If the boiler is old and keeps failing, we explain the options and give a clear price for each.

We handle the plumbing side of boilers and hydronic systems. Where a job needs an oil or gas burner specialist, we say so and tell you what we found. Gas connections and venting are safety items, and we do not guess at them.

Hydronic Heat in Nashua Homes

Some older Nashua homes, including multi-family buildings in Downtown and French Hill, may heat with a boiler and radiators or baseboard. These systems can last a long time but need attention, because radiators, valves and long piping runs add up to many places for a leak or an air pocket.

A boiler that stops in the middle of a cold snap puts the whole plumbing system at risk. Supply lines in exterior walls and unheated spaces can freeze. If a pipe has already split, go to our burst pipe repair page for the emergency steps.

Keep the Boiler Healthy

  • ✓Have the boiler serviced before the heating season, not during the first cold week.
  • ✓Check the pressure gauge now and then. It should sit in the range on the label.
  • ✓Bleed radiators if they are cold at the top.
  • ✓Keep the area around the boiler clear.
  • ✓Do not ignore small leaks. They grow.

We also look at the water heater when a boiler has a tankless coil or an indirect tank. See our water heater repair page for hot water problems.

What to Do Before We Arrive

A boiler is a pressure vessel with a burner, so a few precautions matter more than speed. Gas appliances in the United States are installed and vented under NFPA 54, the National Fuel Gas Code, which is why we check venting and combustion air on every visit.

  • ✓If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. Do not flip switches or use a phone indoors.
  • ✓Make sure a working carbon monoxide alarm is on each level and near sleeping areas.
  • ✓Do not relight a pilot that keeps going out or open the burner cover yourself.
  • ✓Turn off the power switch at the boiler if you see water leaking from it, and keep children and pets away.
  • ✓Note the error code or the noise you hear so we can bring the right parts.
