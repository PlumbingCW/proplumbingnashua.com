Hudson Water, Wells and Permits

Plumbing in Hudson depends on which water system you are on, and local rules shape what we do.

Town water and its source

Hudson Water Utility draws mainly from a well in Litchfield and supplements from the Pennichuck system, treating with chlorine, pH adjustment and phosphate. Town testing reported hardness near 57 ppm, harder than Nashua, so tanks and aerators can collect more scale.

Lead service line inventory

The town completed its lead service line inventory in 2024. If you are curious about your own line, ask the Hudson Water Utility. We replace the private side when a line is bad.

Permits for plumbing work

Hudson plumbing permits go through the Fire Department Inspectional Services Division. A New Hampshire licensed plumber pulls the permit, and the inspector needs 24 hours notice for under-slab, rough-in and final inspections.

Private wells and PFAS

Part of Hudson falls inside the outer boundary NHDES set under the Saint-Gobain consent decree. Private well owners who have not tested can ask NHDES about sampling.
