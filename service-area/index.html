Why We Keep the Area Small

A small area means shorter drives and better arrival windows. It also means we know the water, the sewer, the permits and the housing in each town instead of treating them all the same.

What Changes From Town to Town

Water is the first difference. Nashua is mostly served by Pennichuck, Hudson runs its own water utility with a well in Litchfield and a connection to the Pennichuck system, and Merrimack has two suppliers. Hollis has no municipal water at all. A water heater in a soft water home ages differently from one on harder or private well water, and we plan service intervals to match.

Sewer is the second difference. Nashua and Merrimack run municipal wastewater plants, Hudson has both municipal and private septic, and Hollis homes use private septic. A slow drain in a septic home can mean a full tank or a stressed leach field, so we ask before we assume a clog.

Permits are the third. The City of Nashua Department of Building Safety handles Nashua, and Hudson uses its Fire Department Inspectional Services Division. We ask the office that covers your address.

Reaching You Quickly

Our crews work from 10 Technology Way in Nashua. From there, Hudson is a short drive across the Merrimack River, Merrimack is up the Everett Turnpike and Hollis is west on country roads. We give an honest arrival window when you call, and we call back if it changes.

During a storm or a hard freeze, many people call at once. We prioritize active leaks, sewage backups and no-heat calls, and we tell you where your job sits in the queue.

Where Each Town Fits

Hudson sits across the Merrimack River from Nashua, with Routes 3A, 102 and 111 crossing town and Exit 7 of the Everett Turnpike close by. Most Hudson calls involve town water, older housing near the river and newer subdivisions farther out.

Merrimack runs along the river north of Nashua, with US Route 3 through the commercial center and the Everett Turnpike on the east side. Calls here include restaurants and shops along the highway as well as homes served by Pennichuck or the Merrimack Village District.

Hollis lies west of Nashua, a rural town of farms and conservation land where wells and septic systems are the rule. Calls here often start with a pump, a pressure tank or a slow drain that may be the tank, not the pipe.

In Nashua itself, we work across Downtown, the Tree Streets, French Hill, South Nashua and the Amherst Street corridor, and near the hospitals, campuses and the Millyard. Our commercial plumbing page covers shops and restaurants, and our repiping page covers older houses with worn supply lines.

If You Are Just Outside Our Area

Towns such as Litchfield, Pelham, Bedford, Amherst, Milford and Brookline sit next to the towns we serve. Their ZIP codes, such as 03052, 03076, 03110, 03031, 03055 and 03033, are not part of the area we list as covered, so we do not promise service there. Call with your address and we will tell you honestly whether we can reach you, and when we cannot, we will say so quickly so you can call someone else.

If you are not sure which town your house is in, check your tax bill or your water bill. Some addresses carry a mailing town that differs from the town that issues permits and runs the water utility, and that affects who you call when a problem sits outside your property line.
