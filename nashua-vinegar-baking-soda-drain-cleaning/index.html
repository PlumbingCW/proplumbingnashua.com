Cleaning Drains With Vinegar and Baking Soda in Nashua, NH

Baking soda and vinegar fizz and loosen light buildup in a slow drain, and they smell better than chemical cleaners, but they do not dissolve a real clog. They are fine for a sluggish sink or a smelly drain. They will not clear hair mats, grease blockages or a main line backup, and they do nothing for tree roots or a damaged pipe.

By the Nashua Pro Plumbing team. Published October 23, 2026. Updated November 16, 2026.

What Happens When You Mix Them

Baking soda is a mild base and vinegar is a mild acid. Mixed, they produce carbon dioxide gas and water, which is the fizz. The bubbling can loosen soft film on the walls of a trap, and the hot water flush that follows carries it away. That is all it does. It is a rinse, not a drain machine.

People expect more because the foam looks dramatic. A clog that blocks the pipe is a solid mass. Foam rises above it, not through it, and a full cup of the mix cannot break a hair and soap plug.

How to Do It Properly

1

Clear standing water

Bail the sink or tub so the mix reaches the drain, not sits in a pool.

2

Add baking soda

Pour about half a cup of baking soda into the drain.

3

Add vinegar

Pour half a cup of white vinegar and cover the drain with a plug for a few minutes.

4

Flush with hot water

Run hot tap water, not boiling, for a minute or two.

Do not use boiling water on a PVC trap or a porcelain sink. Hot tap water is enough, and boiling water can soften plastic or crack a fixture.

What It Cannot Do

A hair and soap plug

Foam does not dissolve a plug. Remove the stopper and pull it out by hand or with a small tool.

Grease in a kitchen line

Grease coats the pipe wall. Hot water may move it, and it returns downstream.

Roots or damaged pipe

Neither vinegar nor soda touches roots. A camera shows the truth.

A full backup

If water comes up in other fixtures, stop and call.

Never Mix These With Chemical Cleaners

If you have already used a commercial drain cleaner, do not add vinegar, bleach or anything else. Mixing chemicals in a drain can release harmful fumes. Tell the plumber what you used before they open the line, because chemicals can burn skin on a splash.

Local Note: Soft Water and Pipe Scale in Nashua

Pennichuck Water Works reports core water hardness of about 21 ppm for the Nashua system, which is soft. Soft water leaves less mineral scale on drain walls than hard water does, so slow drains in Nashua homes are more often hair, soap and grease than scale. Vinegar can dissolve some mineral scale, but there is less of it here to dissolve.

Hudson water tests harder, near 57 ppm in the town well, so a vinegar rinse may do more there. Read our Hudson page for what we see across town. In Hollis, private well water varies from home to home, and a septic system dislikes heavy use of any cleaner. Our Hollis page explains why.

Sources: Pennichuck Water Works Consumer Confidence Report, Hudson Water Utility data as reported in the same report.

Better Habits Than Any Cleaner

  • ✓Use a hair catcher in tubs and showers and empty it often.
  • ✓Scrape grease into the trash, not the sink.
  • ✓Run hot water after washing dishes.
  • ✓Do not flush wipes, even ones labeled flushable.
  • ✓Pull and clean pop-up stoppers every few months.
  • ✓Call early. A slow drain is easier to clear than a stopped one.

If a drain keeps slowing, the pipe has a problem a rinse will not fix. Our hydro jetting page covers what we do about repeat clogs.

Other Home Methods Worth Knowing

If the fizz does not help, a few other safe methods can. A plunger works on a sink or tub when the overflow is plugged with a wet rag so pressure goes down the drain. A plastic drain claw pulls hair from a tub or shower in seconds. A hand snake reaches a clog in a branch line. A wet and dry vacuum set to liquid can pull a stubborn blockage from a floor drain.

What we do not recommend is a liquid drain opener. These products can damage older pipe and traps, they sit in a standing clog and become a hazard if you open the trap afterward, and they harm septic systems. If you have used one, tell your plumber.

Signs It Is Not a Simple Clog

A slow tub with a healthy sink is a local clog. A slow drain in every room, gurgling in toilets when you run the washer or a floor drain that bubbles means something larger. In that case more fizz will not help, and waiting lets wastewater back up. Call the 24/7 line and tell us which fixtures are affected, in the order they slowed down.

A Simple Maintenance Routine

If you like the fizz, use it as maintenance, not rescue. Once a month, pour the baking soda and vinegar into drains that you use every day, let them sit, and flush with hot tap water. Follow with a quick check of the stopper area in the bathroom sink, where hair and toothpaste film collect. Wipe the pop-up stopper and rinse it. This takes five minutes and keeps small buildups from turning into clogs.

For kitchen drains, add the habit of running hot water for a minute after greasy dishes. These small habits do more than any product, and they cost nothing.

The Short Version

Vinegar and baking soda are a gentle rinse for light buildup. They will not clear a real clog. If the drain stays slow, use a claw or a snake, avoid chemical openers and call a plumber when more than one fixture is affected.
