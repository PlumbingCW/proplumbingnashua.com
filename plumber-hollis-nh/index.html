Wells, Septic and Plumbing Emergencies

Hollis runs on private wells and private septic systems, according to the state profile for the town. That changes what an emergency looks like. When the water stops, it may be a pump, a tripped breaker, a pressure switch or a leak, and the first question is whether the pressure tank is holding. When a drain backs up, it may be a clog in the house line, a full tank or a stressed leach field.

Our 24/7 line is open, and emergency dispatch is available around the clock for the 03049 ZIP code. We offer same-day appointments when the schedule allows and tell you on the call what we can handle and when a well or septic specialist is the right next step.

Rural homes have a few extra steps. Work through them in order and call us at any point.

Stop the pump

If a pipe has burst, switch off the well pump breaker so it does not keep pushing water out of the break.

Close the house valve

Shut the main valve after the pressure tank to keep stored water in the tank.

Well and Septic Plumbing in Hollis

The state environmental agency reports that radon and arsenic are the most common contaminants in private wells in New Hampshire and recommends testing every three to five years with an accredited laboratory. We do not test water, but we tell you what to ask for and what to do with a filter or a softener once you know the results.

Septic care matters. Do not run a sump pump into the septic tank, keep grease out of drains and watch for slow drains, gurgling and wet spots over the leach field. Our drain cleaning and sewer inspection pages explain how we trace a problem from the house to the tank.

Hollis Services and Local Notes

These are the calls we handle most often in town.

Pressure and pump trouble

Short cycling, low pressure and sputtering faucets can mean a waterlogged tank or a worn pump. We assess the plumbing side and say when the pump needs a well specialist.

Water heaters on well water

Hardness and iron vary by well. Our water heater repair page explains what we check, and we flush tanks on a schedule matched to your water.

Frozen lines and outbuildings

Barns, garages and hose bibs freeze in New Hampshire winters. See our burst pipe repair page.

Old farmhouses

Hollis was incorporated in 1746 and was known as West Dunstable before that. Older homes may have mixed pipe from several eras, and we plan around that.

Around Hollis

The town center sits around Monument Square and Town Hall, with Silver Lake State Beach and the Old Home Days celebration among the local landmarks. Routes 122, 111, 111A and 130 cross town, and the Everett Turnpike is about six miles away at Exit 6, so a call from our Nashua office is a short drive on country roads.

The nearby towns of Amherst, Milford and Brookline lie outside the area we cover, and their ZIP codes are shown only as neighbors. If you are on the edge of Hollis and unsure, call and we will tell you.
