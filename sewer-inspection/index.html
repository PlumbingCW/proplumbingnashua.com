A sewer camera shows us what no cable machine can. We feed a waterproof camera through your line and show you cracks, roots, offsets, sags and blockages on video.

What a Sewer Camera Inspection Shows

A sewer inspection feeds a small, waterproof camera on a flexible rod into the line, usually through a cleanout. The camera records video as it moves and marks distance, so we can tell you where a problem sits and how far it is from the entry point.

Cracks and breaks

Longitudinal cracks, circumferential cracks and holes show on the pipe wall.

Offsets and bellies

A joint that has shifted or a low spot that holds water shows as a step or a pool.

Roots

Fine roots at joints or a dense mass that blocks flow appear on camera.

Scale and grease

Buildup that narrows the pipe shows as a rough, thickened wall.

Collapsed sections

Crushed pipe or missing sections appear as a stop in the line.

Connections

We see where branch lines meet the main and whether the joint looks sound.

When to Schedule an Inspection

  • ✓Before buying a house, especially an older one where the sewer lateral is out of sight and could hide expensive damage.
  • ✓After a repeat clog or a backup, to find out what keeps catching debris.
  • ✓Before a major landscaping or driveway project that could affect the line.
  • ✓Before hydro jetting an older pipe, to confirm it can take the pressure.
  • ✓After a repair, to confirm the work.

A pre-purchase inspection is the most common reason homeowners call us. A camera look at the sewer lateral can tell you whether you are buying a line in good shape or a project. Ask your buyer agent whether it fits your timeline, and call us if it does.
