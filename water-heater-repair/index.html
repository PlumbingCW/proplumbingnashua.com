A Leaking Water Heater Is Urgent

Our 24/7 line is open, so call right away if water is pooling under the tank. While you wait, turn off the cold water supply valve on the pipe above the heater and shut off the power or gas. For an electric unit, switch off its breaker. For gas, turn the gas control to off. Move anything valuable off the floor, and if the water is near an outlet or panel, stay clear and tell us.

A small drip from the temperature and pressure relief valve discharge pipe is different from a failing tank. We sort that out first on every visit, because it changes the repair from a part swap to a conversation about replacement. Night and weekend calls get the same triage as daytime calls.

Common Water Heater Problems We Repair

No hot water

On gas units, often a pilot or thermocouple problem or a failed gas control valve. On electric units, a tripped breaker, a failed heating element or a burned thermostat.

Not enough hot water

A failed lower element, a dip tube that has broken down, or a tank full of sediment that takes up room the water needs.

Rumbling or popping

Sediment baked onto the bottom of the tank. A flush may help early on, but heavy buildup can mean the tank is near the end of its life.

Leaking relief valve

Either the valve has failed or system pressure is too high. Thermal expansion in a closed system is a common cause and needs an expansion tank.

Rusty or smelly water

A depleted anode rod is a common cause. Replacing it can extend the tank if the steel is still sound.

Water too hot or too cool

A thermostat out of range. Temperature is also a safety setting, so we set it to what the manufacturer and local code call for.

Repair or Replace

Most water heater repairs are worth doing if the tank is sound. A thermostat, element, igniter or relief valve is a sensible repair on a unit that is otherwise healthy. A tank that is leaking from the shell, not from a fitting, cannot be patched, and we say so on the spot.

Age is part of the picture, but condition matters more than a number. If the tank is old, repeatedly repaired and showing rust at the base, a water heater replacement usually costs less than chasing the next failure. If you are leaning toward a different kind of unit, our tankless water heater page explains when that choice makes sense.

  • ✓Repair when the tank is sound and the failed part is inexpensive to replace.
  • ✓Replace when the tank leaks from the shell, the base is rusted, or repairs keep stacking up.
  • ✓Ask us to describe both options before you decide. We give a clear price for each.

Water Heaters and Nashua Water

Most of Nashua draws on the Pennichuck Water Works Nashua Core system, which treats water from the Merrimack River and Pennichuck Brook. Pennichuck reports core water hardness of about 21 ppm, which is on the soft side, so heavy scale is less of a driver here than in much of the country. That is good news for tanks and heating elements, but sediment still builds from other sources and the anode rod still wears down.

Hudson has its own water utility and tests harder, so a water heater there may collect more mineral buildup over the same number of years. If you are in Hudson and wondering why a tank is noisy, ask us about it when we come out. The City of Nashua adopts the International Plumbing Code with local amendments, which cover water heater temperature settings and tempering, so we set and test to what applies.

Hybrid and Gas Units Too

We repair gas, electric and hybrid heat pump water heaters. Hybrid units add a compressor, fans and condensate drains that need their own checks, and they need room around them to breathe. We look at the whole installation, not just the failed part.

Safety Steps Before Service

Water heaters store hot water under pressure, and gas models must vent exhaust safely. We follow the manufacturer instructions and NFPA 54 for gas venting and check the temperature and pressure relief valve on every visit.

  • ✓Turn off the gas or the breaker if you see water around the tank or smell gas.
  • ✓Never block or cap the relief valve discharge pipe.
  • ✓Keep flammable liquids and storage away from the heater.
  • ✓Set the thermostat near 120 degrees to reduce scald risk, especially around children and older adults.
