If You Suspect Water Is Running Right Now

Our 24/7 line is open. If you hear running water with every fixture off, see a growing stain or find a warm spot on the floor, shut off the main water valve and call. Then check whether the sound stops. If it does, the leak is somewhere past the meter, and we can find it.

Do not wait on a ceiling stain that keeps spreading. Water finds the lowest path and can travel far from where it escapes the pipe. After-hours, we help you decide by phone whether to wait for a morning visit or send someone now.

How We Find Leaks Without Demolition

1

Meter test

We shut off every fixture and watch the meter. Movement means water is flowing somewhere.

2

Isolate

We close sections of the system to narrow the leak to a line or a zone.

3

Listen and scan

Acoustic listening and thermal imaging pinpoint the spot within a small area.

4

Confirm and report

We confirm the location before any opening and give you repair options.

Pinpointing matters. A well located leak means a small opening instead of a wall of drywall or a trench across the yard.

Where Hidden Leaks Hide

Walls and ceilings

Supply lines and drain connections behind finished surfaces. Signs include stains, bubbling paint and a musty smell.

Slab leaks

Lines under a concrete floor. Signs include warm floor spots, the sound of water and a bill that climbs for no reason.

Crawlspaces and basements

Joints and fittings that drip onto soil or concrete. Signs include damp insulation and rust.

Service line

The line between the street and the house. Signs include a wet patch in the yard and low pressure.

Irrigation and outdoor

Hose bibs, sprinkler lines and pool plumbing. Signs include lush patches and a spinning meter.

Toilets and fixtures

A silent running toilet can waste a surprising volume. A dye test finds it in minutes.

High Water Bill? Start With the Meter

A bill that jumps without a change in habits points to a leak. Locate your water meter, usually in the basement near where the line enters, and read it with every fixture off. If the dial or the low flow indicator moves, water is flowing. If it does not, the bill may reflect the billing period or seasonal use, and we can help you read the statement.

Nashua water is provided by Pennichuck Water Works, and billing questions go to them. We find where the water is going inside your property. If the meter spins and the leak is between the meter and the house, our water service line repair page covers the fix. For leaks that start at a fixture, our toilet repair and faucet repair pages cover the common culprits.
