Emergency Sewer Line Repair in Nashua, NH

Sewage backing up, a gurgling main or a sunken patch in the yard can all mean a damaged sewer line. We find the break on camera, explain your options and repair it by digging, lining or replacement.

If Sewage Is Backing Up Right Now

Our 24/7 line is open, so call at once. While you wait, stop using every fixture and do not flush toilets, run the washer or use the dishwasher. Keep children and pets away from standing wastewater, and open a window if the smell is strong. If the water covers outlets or appliances, stay clear and tell us.

We offer same-day appointments when the schedule allows, and night calls are normal for sewer backups. The first visit usually restores flow and finds the cause. A damaged line is a separate step that we price after we see it on camera.

Signs of a Damaged Sewer Line

Repeated main line backups

Clearing the line helps for a while, then the backup returns. A broken or offset pipe collects debris at the same spot.

Gurgling and slow drains throughout

Several fixtures slow together, especially the lowest ones. Air and water compete in a restricted line.

Sewage odor outdoors

A smell near the foundation or in the yard can mean a leak in the buried line.

Sunken or soggy ground

A wet patch or a dip over the sewer path can mean soil washing into a break.

Roots in the line

Roots enter through joints and cracks. A camera shows where, and cutting them only buys time if the joint stays open.

Cast iron failure

Older cast iron scales inside and can crack or collapse. It is common in pre-1970 homes.

Repair Options

The right repair depends on the camera findings, the depth and the landscape above the pipe. We explain each option with its tradeoffs and let you decide.

Sewer repair methods compared
MethodBest forTradeoff
Spot repair by excavationOne broken section at a known locationOpens the ground at the break only
Full replacement by trenchSeverely damaged, offset or collapsed pipeLargest disruption, longest lasting
Pipe lining, cured in placeCracked or root-damaged pipe that still holds its shapeLess digging, requires a clean, continuous host pipe
Pipe burstingReplacing old pipe along the same pathNeeds entry and exit pits

Pipe lining places a new resin tube inside the old pipe. It works when the host pipe is structurally intact enough to hold its shape and the line is clear. It is not a fix for a collapsed or badly offset section. We look at that on camera before we recommend it. See our sewer inspection page for how that works.

Sewer Lines in Nashua

Nashua has a municipal sewer system and a wastewater treatment plant, so your building sewer connects to the city main at the street. The part on your property is yours to maintain, and the city handles the main. If the camera shows the problem at or past the connection, we tell you so you can contact the city.

The City of Nashua plumbing code, which adopts the International Plumbing Code with local amendments, sets minimum burial depths for building sewers, 48 inches in most cases and 36 inches for septic, with shallower depths possible for insulated pipe at the Code Official approval. We ask the Department of Building Safety whether a repair or replacement needs a permit and an inspection.

In Hollis, where homes use private septic systems, the sewer line runs from the house to the tank. We look at the tank connection and the baffle as part of the job, and we recommend a pumping and inspection if the tank has not been looked at in some time.

What Affects Sewer Repair Price

Sewer repair varies more than most plumbing work. Depth, length, landscape and repair method all matter. We give a clear price before work starts and explain what could change it.

What to Do Before We Arrive

Sewage carries bacteria, and the CDC advises avoiding contact with contaminated water and cleaning surfaces it touches. Treat any backup as a health issue first and a plumbing issue second.

  • ✓Stop using toilets, sinks and the washing machine until we confirm the line is clear.
  • ✓Keep children and pets out of the affected room.
  • ✓Wear gloves and boots if you must handle wet items, and wash well afterward.
  • ✓Do not mix drain cleaners or bleach into a backed up sewer line.
  • ✓Take photos of any damage for your insurance claim.
