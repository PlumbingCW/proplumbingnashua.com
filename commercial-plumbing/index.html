Emergency Commercial Plumbing in Nashua, NH

A plumbing failure can close a restaurant or shut down an office floor. We handle commercial drain backups, leaks, water heaters, restrooms and grease lines, with a line that answers 24/7.

When Plumbing Stops a Business

Our 24/7 line is open for commercial calls. If a restroom is overflowing, a kitchen line is backing up or water is coming through a ceiling, shut the water at the nearest valve, keep customers and staff away from the affected area and call. Tell us the address, the type of business and what is down. Same-day appointments are offered when the schedule allows.

For a restaurant, a backed up floor drain can shut down service in minutes and trigger a health issue. For an office, a failed restroom or water heater affects tenants and the property manager. We triage by impact, so the calls that stop revenue come first.

Commercial Services We Provide

Drain and sewer service

Cable and hydro jetting for floor drains, kitchen lines, restroom groups and main sewers.

Grease trap and interceptor service

Grease traps and interceptors need regular cleaning to prevent backups. We inspect and service small and under-sink units and coordinate larger pump-outs.

Water heaters

Commercial tank and tankless water heaters for restaurants, salons, offices and small facilities.

Restroom plumbing

Flush valves, toilets, urinals, faucets, and slow drains in public and tenant restrooms.

Leaks and pipe repair

Supply line leaks, failed valves and burst pipes in mechanical rooms and ceilings.

Backflow prevention

Backflow assemblies protect the public water supply. We install and repair them and coordinate required testing.

Restaurants and Food Service

Restaurants carry the heaviest drain load. Grease, starch and food scraps collect in kitchen lines and traps. Regular service keeps them clear, and we can set an interval that matches your volume. We pair it with jetting when the line has built up. Our hydro jetting page covers how that works.

Restaurants along Amherst Street, near the Pheasant Lane Mall and Somerset Plaza, and in Downtown Nashua often share old lines with neighbors. When a backup affects several tenants, we help you figure out where the blockage is and who needs to fix it.

Offices, Retail and Multi-Unit Buildings

Offices and retail spaces need steady hot water, working restrooms and quiet fixtures. For multi-unit buildings, we help property managers sort out whether a problem belongs to one unit or the building. Nashua has a mix of older mill-era buildings and newer commercial space, and plumbing age varies widely, from original cast iron stacks to modern PEX. We look at what is actually in the walls.

Around the Nashua Millyard and Downtown, older buildings can have long horizontal runs, multiple tie-ins and unmarked valves. Newer buildings along the Everett Turnpike exits and in the Nashua Technology Park area may have simpler layouts but still need regular service. We work in both.

What to Do Before We Arrive

In a shop, restaurant or office, the first priority is people and product. Model plumbing codes such as the Uniform Plumbing Code and the International Plumbing Code set the baseline for fixture counts, backflow protection and drainage, and the local building office decides which edition and amendments apply to your address.

  • ✓Close the nearest shutoff valve if water is spreading and keep staff away from wet electrical equipment.
  • ✓Do not use drains that are backing up, since sewage is a health hazard.
  • ✓Move stock and records off the floor and take photos for your insurer.
  • ✓Tell us whether the building is leased, because the owner may need to approve repairs.
  • ✓Have the contact for building management ready if we need access to shared risers.
